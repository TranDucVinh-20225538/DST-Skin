# Venue note

The precommit L7 rule reads **MICCAI** on this campaign (`decision_miccai_vs_midl.md`). That verdict stays in the
internal reports as the precommitted readout.

The venue chosen by the authors is **MIDL 2027 (main track)**. Reasons recorded at the time of the choice
(2026-10-07, after the preliminary read):
- the BreakHis ranking criterion passes the locked rule (39/65 rows change winner), but only 2/65 seed-42 rows
  have a CI-significant swap, and FM accuracy on unseen BreakHis patients is 0.60-0.70 (below the 0.8 confound
  flag); the wording is "rankings are unstable", not "flip";
- the Kermany flip and the Kermany Track C drop come from the v3 supplement (a different release), not from new
  patients of the same release (`trackC_clinical_tau/kermany_confound.md`); Kermany is supplementary only;
- dermatology (ISIC 2019, DermaMNIST) shows no material effect.
The L7 verdict is not used in the manuscript.
